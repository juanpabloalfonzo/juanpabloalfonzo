## 🔭 What I'm Working On
### 🌌 Astrophysics: Galaxy Morphology & Bayesian Inference
*   **Katachi-PRIME:** My latest preprint (arXiv:2611.07373) detailing morphological inference of galaxy physical properties using Vision Transformers and Implicit Likelihood Inference.
*   **Katachi (形):** First-author publication in *The Astrophysical Journal* (2024) decoding the imprints of past star formation on present-day morphology with interpretable CNNs.
*   **The Models:** Combining neural density estimators (`sbi`, `ltu-ili`) with mutual information estimators to predict full multidimensional posterior distributions directly from raw optical imaging.

*   ### ⚾ Baseball Analytics: Arsenal Deception
*   **[pitch-tunneling-statcast](#):** An open-source Python repository that models continuous 3D pitch flight trajectories from release point to home plate.
*   **The Math:** Using `pybaseball` to ingest Statcast tracking data, I solve equations of motion to calculate spatial divergence precisely at the batter's commitment decision point (~23.8 ft). 
*   **The Models:** Training gradient-boosted decision trees to evaluate how sequential arsenal deception impacts whiff probability, utilizing SHAP values to isolate the marginal impact of late-break efficiency on batter outcomes.
